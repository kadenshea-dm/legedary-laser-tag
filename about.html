Meet the team behind the glow

More Than Just a Game

Neon Strike Laser Tag was imagined by three friends who wanted to create a place where people could unplug from their routines, challenge themselves, and have fun together. Our fictional arena brings that idea to life through glowing scenery, team missions, and a welcoming atmosphere.

Our Story

The idea began when Jordan Ellis, Maya Brooks, and Ethan Cole organized a laser tag night for their friends. They loved how quickly strangers became teammates and how each match brought out a different mix of strategy, confidence, and laughter.

They imagined Neon Strike as a place for all kinds of players: first-timers learning the equipment, competitive friends chasing a high score, and families making memories together. The result is a fictional entertainment business built around a simple idea: the best games are the ones everyone can enjoy.

Meet Our Founders

Jordan Ellis — Arena Director

Jordan loves designing missions that reward communication and creative thinking. Jordan's goal is to make every round feel exciting, whether players are brand new or already planning their next strategy.

Maya Brooks — Guest Experience Lead

Maya focuses on helping guests feel comfortable, welcomed, and ready to play. From explaining the rules to organizing group visits, Maya believes a great experience begins before the first match.

Ethan Cole — Game Operations Lead

Ethan enjoys the technical side of the arena, from checking equipment to developing fun team challenges. Ethan works to keep the action organized and the game moving.

Our Shared Mission

We want players to leave feeling energized, connected, and eager to come back. Respect, teamwork, and friendly competition are at the center of every Neon Strike mission.

What Makes Us Different?

  • Team-focused play: Winning takes communication, cooperation, and smart decisions.
  • A welcoming atmosphere: New players can learn the basics before stepping into a match.
  • Reasons to return: Rotating mission themes and weekly specials keep each visit interesting.
  • Celebrations with a twist: Groups can make their next birthday or get-together an active experience.
