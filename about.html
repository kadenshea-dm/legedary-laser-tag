More Than Just a Game

At Neon Strike Laser Tag, strategy meets teamwork in a neon-powered world of friendly competition. We're all about bringing people together for memorable adventures.

How It All Started

✦

A Bright Idea

Neon Strike Laser Tag began with a simple idea from fictional founder Jordan Ellis: create a place where friends, families, and teammates could step away from everyday routines and share an exciting adventure.

◎

Built for Adventure

With fictional co-founder Maya Chen helping shape the vision, Neon Strike grew into a concept centered on immersive lighting, strategic gameplay, friendly rivalries, and unforgettable group experiences.

What We Believe In

⚡

Excitement for Everyone

Every game is an opportunity to try something new, challenge yourself, and have fun with people you enjoy.

✧

Teamwork Wins

Communication, strategy, and cooperation make every match more rewarding, whether you're playing with friends or meeting new teammates.

★

Friendly Competition

We believe the best competition combines big moments, good sportsmanship, and plenty of laughs.

◇

Memorable Moments

From birthday celebrations to group outings, the goal is to create stories worth sharing long after the game ends.
