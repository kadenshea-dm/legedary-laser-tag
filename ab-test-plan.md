# A/B Test Plan – Neon Strike Laser Tag

## 1. Page and Element Being Tested

The experiment will test the homepage (`index.html`) of the Neon Strike Laser Tag website.

The single element being changed is the wording of the main call-to-action button in the homepage hero section.

- **Version A:** "Explore Parties & Events"
- **Version B:** "Plan Your Laser Tag Adventure"

Both buttons will link to the same Parties & Events page. All other page content, images, colors, layout, and links will remain identical.

## 2. Hypothesis and Reasoning

I hypothesize that Version B will produce a higher percentage of visitors who say they would click the main call-to-action button.

The wording "Plan Your Laser Tag Adventure" may be more effective because it gives visitors a clear action and makes the experience sound more engaging. Version A describes a section of the website, while Version B focuses on what the visitor can do.

## 3. Model, Persona, Prompt, and Assignment

**Model:** GPT-6. I will use the same model and settings for every trial and record the exact model/version used.

**Persona:** A college student in Iowa City who is looking for a fun activity to do with friends.

**Prompt:** 

"You are a college student in Iowa City looking for a fun activity to do with a group of friends. You are visiting the Neon Strike Laser Tag website to decide whether it is something you would be interested in. Review the homepage provided below. Would you click the main call-to-action button to learn more about the party options? Answer YES or NO, followed by one sentence explaining your decision.

[Insert Version A or Version B homepage here.]"

Each trial will take place in a new conversation with no memory of previous trials. The same persona and prompt will be used every time, with only the homepage version changing.

I will randomly assign 50 trials to Version A and 50 trials to Version B. I will prepare and record the assignments before conducting the trials.

## 4. Primary Outcome and Measurement

The primary outcome will be whether the AI visitor says YES or NO when asked if they would click the main call-to-action button.

A YES will count as a positive response, and a NO will count as a negative response.

I will calculate the percentage of positive responses for each version.

## 5. Sample Size and Stopping Rule

The experiment will include 100 total trials:

- Version A: 50 trials
- Version B: 50 trials

I will complete all 100 trials before analyzing the results. I will not stop early based on the results.

The experiment will be launched no later than November 17, 2026, with data collection completed before the final project deadline.

## 6. Comparison and Expected Results

I will compare the percentage of YES responses between Version A and Version B.

The difference will be calculated by subtracting Version A's positive response percentage from Version B's positive response percentage.

A higher positive response percentage for Version B would support my hypothesis. I will also conduct a statistical test to determine whether the observed difference could reasonably be due to chance.

Even if Version B performs better, the results will only show how the AI visitors responded. They cannot establish how actual customers would behave.
