How to Plan Your Event

  1. Choose your occasion. Decide whether you are celebrating a birthday, planning a family outing, or organizing a group event.
  2. Estimate your group size. Make a guest list and consider the ages and experience levels of your players.
  3. Pick your activities. Think about team challenges, multiple rounds, and time to gather between matches.
  4. Confirm the details. For a real booking, a host would need to confirm availability, pricing, participant requirements, and any food or room arrangements.

What Should Guests Bring?

Comfortable Clothing

Wear clothes that allow you to move easily, along with closed-toe shoes suitable for active play.

A Team Mindset

Encourage players to communicate, follow arena instructions, and respect their teammates and opponents.

A Sense of Adventure

No previous laser tag experience is needed in this fictional arena. New players can learn the rules before their first mission.
