Seasonal Challenges

Squad Score Challenge

Bring your friends, pick a team name, and see how well you work together. The imagined challenge encourages players to communicate, try different strategies, and celebrate team achievements rather than focusing only on individual scores.

First-Time Player Mission

New to laser tag? This themed experience gives first-time players a chance to learn the equipment, understand the rules, and build confidence before joining a more competitive round.

Family Team Challenge

Families can split into teams and test their coordination through a series of friendly missions. The focus is on participation, cooperation, and making memories together.

How to Make the Most of Your Visit

  • Check the featured theme before planning your outing.
  • Bring comfortable clothing and closed-toe shoes for active play.
  • Arrive with enough time to learn the rules and prepare your team.
  • Keep an open mind and try a different strategy in each match.
